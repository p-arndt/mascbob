New `grab` reaction: hold the head, an arm or a leg and pull it around like a puppet. The head moves on a stretchy neck and tilts into the pull, arms bend at the elbow and stretch like rubber past their reach, legs swing out at the hip and stretch, and the whole figure leans into the pull. Everything snaps back with a wobble when let go. A plain click is still a boop. It is on by default and fires `onreaction` with `{ type: "grab", part }`; in head-only mode only the head can be grabbed.
